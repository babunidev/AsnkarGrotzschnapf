# Anskar Grotzschnapf — Artistic and Publication Design

**Human project author:** Babuni.dev. **Fictional artist:** Anskar Grotzschnapf. Original poets and translators retain authorship of their words.

The experience is an ongoing fictional artist rather than a one-shot image prompt. Each daily work starts from a cleared *complete poem*; a local language-model interpretation generates a visual prompt, and local `sd-cli` produces a 1600×1600 image. The source poem and the visual prompt must remain separate records. A print pack prepares the image for downloadable art, while publication extends the persona across Instagram and, when ready, Etsy.

The design distinguishes the original image, a preview, detail crops and the full-resolution paid print. Task 021 records that Etsy listings show previews and detail crops; the full print PNG is reserved for the download. Distribution does not transfer poem copyright or eliminate the need for accurate poet/translator/source attribution.

The user-facing rhythm is once per Swedish day. The fiction of a daily artist depends on credible continuity: interrupted work resumes rather than inventing a second piece, and remote publication failures must not silently erase eligible source material. The actual final images, print quality, social presentation and storefront need review in their respective environments; this audit did not generate or publish any art.
