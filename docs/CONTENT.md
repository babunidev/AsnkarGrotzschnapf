# Anskar Grotzschnapf — Content, Poetry and Publication Provenance

**Author:** Babuni.dev. **Artist persona:** Anskar Grotzschnapf.

## Creative inputs

The local archive (`data/archive.sqlite`) stores poem records and clearance metadata. Poets and translators are separately credited. A poem may only enter the daily selection pool when eligibility rules are satisfied. The original poem is preserved as source text; its derived image prompt is not a substitute for that text. Task 015 documents an exact-text mismatch caused by stripping leading indentation from a Walt Whitman poem and the subsequent identity-based recovery.

## Produced outputs

The daily pipeline records eligible selection, visual interpretation, a locally generated 1600×1600 artwork, print-pack manifests and downstream publication states. The print PNG is distinguished from marketplace preview images and detail crops. An Instagram post may contain the artwork with the original poem according to the project publishing rules; an Etsy quantity-one listing occurs only when the shop is ready.

## Publication policy

Track poem ID, exact source text, author/translator, rights status, generated art model and prompt, output and pack IDs, publication status and any third-party credits. Do not publish secrets, tokens, customer information, unpublished source texts lacking clearance, or misleading claims that AI wrote the original poetry. The currently active scheduler, storefront and integrations were not independently checked during this audit.
