# Anskar Grotzschnapf — concept

**Project author:** Babuni.dev  
**Fictional artist:** Anskar Grotzschnapf

Anskar is an autonomous fictional artist implemented as a local-first daily creative system. Each run selects an eligible poem from a curated archive, turns it into a visual interpretation, produces print-ready artwork, and prepares or publishes it through Instagram and Etsy. The point is the ongoing art experiment, not a claim that software authored the original poems.

The distinction between the human who designed the artwork system, its fictional persona, and the writers/translators of source poetry is essential. Those attributions are kept separately in project records.
